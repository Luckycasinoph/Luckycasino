WORKS


I created this project to encourage students to choose the ICT strand by explaining key ideas in graphic design, like symmetry, balance, color, and emphasis. These concepts help create designs that are clear and visually appealing. The project invites students to explore ICT, where they can learn skills like making websites, coding apps, and understanding cybersecurity, helping them succeed in the digital world.

This picture, created as part of my project and posted on my Facebook account, was taken at Kaunlaran High School. It shows the school as an important part of the community, where students learn, gain knowledge, and build a better future. The image uses techniques like saturation, highlights, brightness, and contrast to enhance it. Saturation brightens colors, highlights add depth, brightness improves clarity, and contrast defines light and dark areas, making the picture more engaging. This is our page, Safety First Hub. Our mission is to help people stay safe by raising awareness about accidents and disasters. We share tips and advice on how to stay safe at home, work, or in public. Our goal is to help people be more prepared and reduce risks in everyday life. Thanks to platforms like Facebook, we can easily share this information online. These tools also let us use images and videos to make our content more interesting and helpful. With the help of ICT, sharing such information has become faster and more accessible.
